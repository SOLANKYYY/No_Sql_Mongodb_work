# Experiment 05 · Sales aggregation and collection joins

Calculate revenue from quantity and unit price, group orders into value bands, and join orders to a separate product catalogue.

## Findings and evidence

Of 20 orders, two had a value from 50 up to 200 and 18 fell in the large-order bucket. The filtered join keeps completed orders from the first quarter of 2024 with at least two units.
